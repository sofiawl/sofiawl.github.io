\section{About}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{2.8cm}X@{}}
    {\small\color{muted}\textit{How I got here}} &
    \small I came to computer science when I discovered the work of \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_Crespo}{Sofia Crespo},
    an artist who weaves together computation, art and ecology. That encounter sent me
    down a long path of research into different technology fields, and I realised that
    what truly fulfils me is studying hard problems and proposing creative solutions,
    something common to all of them. The challenge of entering a field I knew nothing
    about, and knowing it would transform the way I see the world, made the decision easy.
\end{tabularx}
\vspace{0.12em}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{2.8cm}X@{}}
    {\small\color{muted}\textit{The present}} &
    \small Artificial Intelligence and Computational Vision, and everything that
    intersects them, from vision-language models to the architectures underneath,
    are what excite me most; that intersection feels like where everything I want
    to build converges. I am equally drawn to research and to technical leadership,
    guiding a team's architecture and decisions, aligning people around a problem,
    and turning research into something that ships. Outside of research, I am an avid reader and I am committed to social and community work. I enjoy a collaborative environment with
    people from different fields; I learn as much from conversation and shared
    experience with them as from any paper.
\end{tabularx}
\vspace{0.12em}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{2.8cm}X@{}}
    {\small\color{muted}\textit{Where I am going}} &
    \small I intend to pursue a Master's degree and a PhD, and/or study whatever
    complementary field the project I am developing demands, while also building the
    experience to one day work as a tech lead. My long-term goal is to create
    something with genuine social and ecological impact in computer science, and to
    choose problems where that impact is measurable, not assumed. 
\end{tabularx}
